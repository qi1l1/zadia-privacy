This Privacy Policy explains how ZADIA collects, uses, stores and protects personal information when you use the ZADIA mobile application.

By using ZADIA, you acknowledge this policy. Where consent is required by applicable law, we will seek that consent separately.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, ZADIA may process:

  • Account information: Your full name, email address and authentication information when you register or sign in.

  • Training and workout information: Training plans, exercise records, workout history, training preferences and performance records you enter.

We aim to collect and process information that is reasonably necessary to provide the application's features.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account.

Users should protect their account credentials and contact us if they suspect unauthorised account access.

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application, maintain relevant records, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal requirements.
